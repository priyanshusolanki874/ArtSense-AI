About ArtSense AI

ArtSense AI is an AI-powered artwork evaluation platform that helps artists receive instant feedback on their creative work. Upload your artwork and get an AI-generated score, strengths, weaknesses, and suggestions for improvement.

Our Mission🎯

To help artists improve their skills using Artificial Intelligence by providing quick, accurate, and constructive feedback.

Features🚀

  • Upload Artwork
  • AI Artwork Analysis
  • Artwork Score
  • Strengths Detection
  • Weaknesses Detection
  • Improvement Suggestions

Tech Stack🛠

  • Django
  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • SQLite
  • OpenRouter API
